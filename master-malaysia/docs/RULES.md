# Master Malaysia - Official Rules

## How to Play the Game

1. **Shuffle the cards** - 4 Standard and 1 Risk (General) Question Packs, place them on board to indicate the colours (Green - LAND, Red - PEOPLE, Yellow - HERITAGE, Blue - CULTURE)
2. Each player choose a colour token according to the Sash colour on his side of the board.
3. Place each token on Step 1.
4. Player 1 will have the green token followed by the next player in a clockwise direction (Player 2 - red, Player 3 - Yellow, Player 4 - blue). When only 2 players are engaged, they must start with Green/Yellow, and proceed to red/blue upon completion.
5. Player 1 will throw the dice.
6. The player opposite will take a card from the Question Pack and pose to P1.
7. If P1 answers correctly, the total dice score plus a bonus of 120 points and another 20 points (as indicated in Step 1) will be given to P1. If answer is incorrect, no points are given.
8. The score is recorded play moves to P2.
9. P2 throws dice and follow the same instruction from 5 to 8.
10. Play proceeds to P3 and P4. Once a round has been completed, the play returns to P1, who then **moves his token to Step 2** and follows the same procedure as before.
11. Play continues in this fashion but at Step 6, 8, 18, 20, 30, 32, 42, 44 and at the four corner areas (PURPLE), the players have the Option to answer the Risk (General) questions.
12. Players can also choose not to take the RISK of answering the General Questions and proceed to the next step.
13. Once all players have reached Step 48, each player may choose to enter the last RISK Area or end the game at Step 48. Scorekeeper will then total all the points and announce the winner.

*NOTE: The same procedure applies to the Elementary Level which comprises two sets of Elementary Questions (grey and black colour cards).*

## Developer Notes
* **Movement:** Players DO NOT move based on the dice roll. The dice is ONLY used for calculating points and triggering specific tile rules. Players move exactly 1 step sequentially each round. 
* **The Board Layout (52 Spaces):** The physical board consists of 48 numbered Steps and 4 unnumbered Corner Risk Areas. The game consists of 52 rounds per player. The spaces are sequenced as: Steps 1-12, Corner 1, Steps 13-24, Corner 2, Steps 25-36, Corner 3, Steps 37-48, Corner 4.
* **Base Score:** The base score for a correct answer is solely the Dice Roll. There is no 120 base point.

## Points System & Board Definitions

There are 2 types of points available:
1. **Conditional points:** These points can only be awarded if the question is answered correctly and when the condition outlined at the step is fulfilled.
2. **Bonus points:** These points ie. 10 points at even steps and 20 points at odd steps, are awarded if the questions is answered correctly.

### Tile Glossary
*   **STEPS 1,10,16,19,26,33,43 - CORRECT + 'Y'** : A correct answer allows you to collect +'Y' points.
*   **STEPS 5,9,23,31,45,46,47 - INCORRECT - 'Y'** : An incorrect answer results in a penalty of 'Y' points (minus 'Y' points from your score)
*   **STEPS 3,15,27,34,39 - DOUBLES** : Both dice have the same score when thrown, eg. 2 & 2 etc.
*   **STEPS 13,25,37 - SCORE > 'Z'** : Collect Bonus points 'Y', if total score on score sheet is more than the figure on the board.
*   **STEPS 4,11,17,22,29,41 - DICE SCORE Less Than < 'X' OR More Than > 'X'** : For a dice score of Less Than (<) OR More Than (>) 'X' results in a penalty OR collection of 'Y' points (minus/plus 'Y' points from/to score).
*   **STEPS 2,7,14,21,28,35,38,40 - DICE SCORE 'X' + 'Y'** : If you answer the question correctly for a dice score of 'X' you will get 'Y' points. If you do not get the required score you will only get the bonus points.
*   **STEPS 12,24,36,48 - ALL CORRECT ANSWERS** : If you answered correctly from Steps 1-12, 13-24, 25-36, OR 37-48 THEN collect 'Y' points.
*   **RISK AREAS (Steps 6, 8, 18, 20, 30, 32, 42, 44, and 4 Corners)** : Collect points for a correct answer. Minus points for incorrect answer.
