STEP 4 Active Defense: Dodge vs. Perfect Parry

During the enemy turn, combat is NOT passive! When the CPU winds up an attack, a sweeping meter appears with two active defensive options:

🛡️ DODGE [SPACEBAR]

Safe Evasion (Wide window)

Leaps backward. Takes 0 DMG, but deals 0 return damage.

⚔️ PARRY [KEY P]

High Risk, High Reward (Sweet spot)

Negates all damage, grants +1 AP, and unleashes an immediate COUNTER-STRIKE (50-80 DMG)!

💡 PRO TIP: If you're low on HP, use DODGE to stay safe. If you want to end the fight quickly, time a PARRY in the golden zone to counter-attack!
